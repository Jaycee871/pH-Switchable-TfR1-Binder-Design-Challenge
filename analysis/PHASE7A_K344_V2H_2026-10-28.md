# Phase 7A — K344-facing V2H mutants: full human/mouse pose validation

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Successful validation run:** GitHub Actions 40695773854  
**Parent scaffold:** compact RFdiffusion seed 20261102 (57 aa)  
**Question:** can an explicitly engineered V2H substitution at binder position 2 preserve the intended apical TfR1 pose while creating a plausible acidic-pH repulsion mechanism against conserved human K344 / mouse K346?

## Why this experiment was run

Phase 6 showed that broad, strong histidine bias can force histidines into the interface but saturates the sequence and produces an N-terminal poly-His artifact. Phase 6B showed that distributed +2.0/+1.3 histidine bias falls back to 0/24 H, and Phase 6C showed that even +4.0 at position 2 alone still returns V at position 2 in 24/24 ProteinMPNN sequences.

Rather than increasing the bias further, Phase 7A treated **V2H as an explicit engineered point-mutation hypothesis** and tested the resulting structures directly.

Two low-ProteinMPNN-score parents were used:

- **TFR1_K344_V2H_03**: `AHEENEKELEKKKKKEIKEAIKKGKEKIEELEKEYKKEKESLEELKKKMEEEEKLMK`
- **TFR1_K344_V2H_05**: `AHEENRKELEKKKKKEVEKAKKEGEEKIEEIEKEYKKEIESLEKLENRMKEEEKAQK`

The mutation is not claimed to have been selected by ProteinMPNN.

## Pose-gate result

Both mutants **fail decisively in both species**.

| Candidate | Species | protein ipTM | Predicted interface | Preferred apical core retained | Pose gate |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| V2H_03 | human | 0.183 | ~507, 525-651 | No | FAIL |
| V2H_03 | mouse | 0.169 | ~514, 527-657 | No | FAIL |
| V2H_05 | human | 0.143 | ~511, 523-654 | No | FAIL |
| V2H_05 | mouse | 0.147 | ~514, 526-657 | No | FAIL |

The strict gate required:

- protein ipTM >= 0.50;
- at least one preferred apical-core contact;
- an actual contact in the intended apical region.

None of the four predictions satisfies even the epitope-location requirement.

## Important interpretation

The high overall complex pLDDT values (~0.81-0.92) do **not** rescue these designs. The chains are individually well folded, but the relative docking pose is not trusted:

- protein ipTM remains only 0.14-0.18;
- complex ipDE is ~16-17 Å;
- the interface again relocates to the same distal ~507-657 region seen in the earlier C57/A29 validation rounds.

This reproduces the project's central failure mode under a targeted V2H perturbation.

## Decision

**Close the seed-20261102 / C57 lane as a primary design route.**

The scaffold remains useful as a negative-control history for the project, but further ProteinMPNN histidine tuning or point-mutation work on this backbone is not a good use of Boltz-2 budget unless a substantially different structural constraint method becomes available.

The main design effort moves to the independent **apical-domain-only RFdiffusion Kaggle lane**, which is now guarded by a strict conserved-contact/risk/compactness gate before sequence design.

## What this experiment does and does not show

It shows:

- the V2H mutation does not rescue the full-ectodomain pose;
- pose collapse is robust to this local sequence perturbation;
- the old scaffold should be deprioritized.

It does **not** show:

- that V2H is intrinsically a bad pH-switch mechanism;
- that K344-facing histidine engineering is invalid on a different scaffold;
- any direct pH 6.0 binding result.

A pH switch should only be engineered after a scaffold first survives the human/mouse apical pose gate.
